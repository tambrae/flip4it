Common questions

How do I change the coin's faces?
Tap the pencil button at the top of the screen, type an emoji or a word for each side, then tap Done.

How do I save a pair?
Tap the list button, then tap Save to keep the current pair. Tap any saved pair to use it again.

How do I delete a saved pair?
In the saved pairs list, swipe left on the pair and tap Delete.

Is the flip fair?
Yes. Every flip is a true 50/50 using your iPhone's built-in random number generator.

Does the app collect my data?
No. Your faces and saved pairs stay on your phone. See the privacy policy.
