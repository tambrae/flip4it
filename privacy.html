Privacy Policy

Flip 4 It · Last updated 1 October 2026

Flip 4 It is made by Tambrae Maynard. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: it doesn't collect any.

Information we collect

None. Flip 4 It does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-up and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

The coin faces you create and the pairs you save are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes them.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics or third-party services, and does not connect to the internet.

Children

Because the app collects no information, it is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email flip4it.app@gmail.com.
